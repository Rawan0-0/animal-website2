Viruses

  • Did you know that viruses are not made of cells, yet they infect cells in animals
  • They can change an animal's DNA by copying their genetic material into the animal's DNA, or they can damage the cells
  • If the virus causes changes in the reproductive cells of an animal, they can be passed to the animal's offspring

    Abit of chemistry!

  • A DNA can be pictured as a long chain made of nucleotides, with the bases arranged in a particular order. That order is the genetic sequence.
  • A molecule is two or more atoms joined together. Atoms are tiny building blocks that make up matter; water, for example, is a molecule made of hydrogen and oxygen atoms.
  • A nucleotide is a small molecule that acts as one building block of DNA or RNA. It has three parts: a sugar, a phosphate group, and a chemical base—the “letter” that carries genetic information.
