Viruses

  • Did you know that viruses are not made of cells, yet they infect cells in animals?
  • They can change an animal's DNA by copying their genetic material into it, or they can damage the cells.
  • If a virus causes changes in an animal's reproductive cells, those changes can be passed to its offspring.

A bit of chemistry!

  • A DNA molecule can be pictured as a long chain made of nucleotides.
  • A molecule is two or more atoms joined together.
  • A nucleotide is a building block of DNA or RNA.

What does this have to do with viruses?

First, you need to understand this process

A nucleotide contains a sugar, a phosphate group, and a chemical base. Nucleotides join into a strand through chemical bonds.

Why am I telling you this?

Viruses can be re-engineered. Think of it like reverse engineering: the genetic material is changed.
